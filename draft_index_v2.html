Profile Overview

PhD candidate at the MIST Lab, Polytechnique Montréal, and Mila Quebec AI Institute, supervised by Giovanni Beltrame and co-supervised by Jana Pavlasek. My research lies at the intersection of deep learning, robotics and reinforcement learning, with a current focus on Visual Navigation Models (VNMs): RGB-only navigation, topological graphs, learned distance prediction and cross-embodiment generalization for robust deployment on real robots.

My work spans safe reinforcement learning (control barrier functions), model training and real-world evaluation, and deployment on physical platforms such as Boston Dynamics Spot, AgileX Bunker, AgileX Limo and Unitree G1. In 2026 I demonstrated VISTA running live on a Unitree G1 humanoid for the Italian Embassy and the Italian Navy, and presented it to Airbus R&T leadership.

Before research, I spent three years as an apprentice DevOps / production engineer at Orange. I was also a core team member of Women in AI & Robotics in Montréal and lead an educational initiative on AI and robotics at McGill University.

Live Demonstrations & Industry Presentations

Taking research out of the lab: presenting and demonstrating my work to institutional, industry and research audiences, often alongside collaborators from other labs and backgrounds.

Live Spot Demo at Mila — VISTA & FARM Prompt-Based Navigation

September 14, 2026 Live robot demo Boston Dynamics Spot Cross-lab collaboration

At Mila – Quebec AI Institute, I co-ran a live demonstration on the Boston Dynamics Spot robot featuring two navigation systems:

  • VISTA, my scale-aware visual navigation model.
  • FARM, an interface for prompt-based navigation with Spot. Using a pre-collected metric map, users ask the robot in natural language to go to specific places. FARM was developed by Ria Aurora, a former Master's student in Liam Paull's lab at Université de Montréal. I supported the demo by introducing Spot's basics and operation.

Collaboration: this demo brought together work from two labs and two research backgrounds (learned visual navigation and language-driven, map-based navigation) on one shared robot. That kind of collaboration runs through my work, from co-leading VISTA with a dataset-focused researcher to supervising interns from very different starting points (see Supervision).

Robot Deployment on the Canadian Space Agency's Mars-Like Terrain

September 29, 2025 Field deployment 2-day test Planetary analog terrain

In Montréal, we spent two days at the Canadian Space Agency, which has a real-world, Mars-like terrain with craters, sand hills and rocks. This was mostly a test for ourselves: we wanted to see how visual navigation models behave in an environment very different from the ones they were trained on.

Radio-Canada Field Mission — Spot & Drone in the Trou du Diable Cave

Date TBD Field deployment Boston Dynamics Spot Cave exploration Media coverage

For a Radio-Canada field mission, our team sent a Spot robot carrying a drone on its back into Trou du Diable, a cave in Quebec. I was there in a support role, helping the rest of the team on site.

When the water level in the cave rose, Maëlle Mathieu (Master's student) and I improvised a waterproofing fix for Spot's exposed electronics from tape and plastic. With very limited resources, it held, and the team deployed Spot successfully even where the water was very high.
