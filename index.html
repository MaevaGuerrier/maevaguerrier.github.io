VISTA: Scale-Aware Visual Navigation

Problem
Camera-only navigation models lose track of scale and break when moved to a new robot or environment.
Built
Co-led (equal contribution) a navigation model conditioned on action history with a DINOv3 encoder, and took it from training to deployment on physical robots.
Result
100% zero-shot goal-prediction accuracy in outdoor, forest and office deployments. Demonstrated live on a Unitree G1 humanoid and Boston Dynamics Spot.

Real-World Evaluation of Navigation Foundation Models

Problem
Success rate alone hides why navigation models fail on real hardware.
Built
Led a zero-shot evaluation of 5 models (GNM, ViNT, NoMaD, NaviBridger, CrossFormer) on 2 robots across 5 environments, with path-quality, goal-recognition and perturbation-robustness metrics.
Result
Open-source evaluation code and concrete lessons on where current models fail, for anyone choosing a model to deploy.
