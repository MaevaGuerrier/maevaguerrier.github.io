Spot & Drone Cave Exploration — Radio-Canada Field Mission

Date TBD Field deployment Boston Dynamics Spot Cave exploration Media coverage

For a Radio-Canada field mission, our team took a Boston Dynamics Spot robot into Trou du Diable, a cave in Quebec. Spot carried a drone on its back: the goal was for the pair to explore the cave together, with Spot moving through the passages and the drone ready to take off where the legged robot could not go.

I was there in a support role, helping the other team members prepare, run and recover the robots on site.

Waterproofing Spot on the spot

The most memorable moment came from the cave itself: the water level had risen, and parts of the route Spot needed to cross were much deeper than planned. Spot's exposed electronics were not built for that.

With very limited resources on hand, Maëlle Mathieu (Master's student) and I improvised a fix using tape and plastic sheeting to seal the exposed electronics. It held up: the team deployed Spot successfully in the cave, even where the water was very high.

  • Supported the team throughout the field deployment in a demanding, wet, low-light environment.
  • Co-designed an improvised waterproofing solution for Spot from tape and plastic.
  • Enabled a successful deployment despite unexpectedly high water in the cave.
