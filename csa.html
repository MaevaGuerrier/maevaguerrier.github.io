Robot Deployment at the Canadian Space Agency

September 29, 2025 Field deployment 2-day test Planetary analog terrain

The Canadian Space Agency (CSA) has a real-world, Mars-like outdoor terrain with craters, sand hills and rocks. We spent two days there deploying our robot and visual navigation models on it.

This was mostly an internal test, not a public demo. We wanted to see how the models behave in an environment very far from where they were trained: loose sand, uneven slopes, scattered rocks, and few of the visual landmarks you find in indoor and urban scenes.
